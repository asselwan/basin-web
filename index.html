Your exact amounts stay on your device.

Basin processes exact transaction amounts on your Android device. It does not send those amounts to Basin's servers.

If you opt in to spend benchmarks, only an anonymous bracketed range leaves the device, never a real number. If you use Ask, Basin sends a summary of your spending and your question to answer it, never the raw transaction list.
